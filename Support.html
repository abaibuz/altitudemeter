Altitude Meter Support

Altitude Meter displays altitude, GPS accuracy, and atmospheric pressure on supported iPhone and Apple Watch devices.

Measurement unavailable

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services and allow Altitude Meter to use your location while the app is in use. Move outdoors with a clear view of the sky and wait for a valid GPS measurement.

Pressure unavailable

Pressure requires a physical device with a supported barometer. It may not be available in Simulator or on unsupported hardware.

Widget not updating

Open Altitude Meter to capture a new measurement. iOS and watchOS control widget refresh scheduling, so the displayed value may be the most recent locally saved measurement.

Measurement limitations

Readings depend on GPS reception, device hardware, weather, and atmospheric conditions. Do not use the app for aviation, emergencies, surveying, or safety-critical navigation.
