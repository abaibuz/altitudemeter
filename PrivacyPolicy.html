Altitude Meter Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Overview

Altitude Meter measures altitude, GPS vertical accuracy, and atmospheric pressure on supported Apple devices. The app does not create user accounts, display advertising, track users, or sell personal data.

Location and sensor data

The app requests access to location services to calculate altitude above sea level. It uses the device barometer to improve short-term altitude measurements and to display atmospheric pressure. These measurements are processed on the device and are not transmitted to the developer or to third parties.

Widgets and Apple Watch

The latest altitude, pressure, accuracy, and update time may be stored locally in an Apple App Group so that the app's widgets and Apple Watch complications can display them. This information remains within the app's private group container.

Data collection and sharing

The developer does not collect, transmit, share, or sell personal data. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Measurements stored for widgets remain on the device. You can remove them by deleting the app and its related widgets from your devices.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
