## Description

Altitude Meter gives you a clear view of your current elevation and atmospheric pressure.

The app combines GPS altitude with relative barometer changes to provide a stable, responsive reading. View the latest measurement in the iPhone app, on Apple Watch, in Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, or as a complication on your watch face.

Features:

- Current altitude above sea level
- Atmospheric pressure in kPa
- GPS vertical accuracy
- Complementary filtering for smoother readings
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
- Native Apple Watch app
- Apple Watch complications
- Local processing with no accounts, ads, or analytics

Altitude and pressure measurements depend on device hardware, GPS availability, and atmospheric conditions. The app is not intended for aviation, emergency response, surveying, or safety-critical navigation.

## App Review notes

Altitude Meter uses When In Use location access solely to calculate altitude above sea level. It uses CMAltimeter to display atmospheric pressure and stabilize short-term altitude changes. Measurements remain on the device. An App Group stores only the latest altitude, pressure, vertical accuracy, and timestamp so the included widgets and Apple Watch complications can display them. The app contains no accounts, advertising, analytics, or third-party SDKs.

To test, launch the app, allow location access, and wait for a valid GPS altitude. Pressure requires a supported physical device with a barometer. Simulator values may be unavailable.
